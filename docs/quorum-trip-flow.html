    Why nobody has to front the group.

    Three money checks

    Before the voteEach payer’s quote per head ≤ organiser’s budget × (1 + overshoot), and ≤ their own ceiling. This is the only place overshoot applies.
    After the voteEach yes-voter’s share (their part of the stay plus their own travel) must fit their heads × their own per-head ceiling. Rooms and legs are resized for that group.
    Before bookingEach payer blocks their cap: heads × their own ceiling, not rounded up. Headroom is cap minus what has been charged. They are charged only when booking begins.

    The rail reality check

    Pine Labs today: capture payer by payer. If one debit fails, refund earlier debits, release remaining holds and enter LAPSED before paying suppliers.

    The ask: one atomic, multi-payer group order with escrow. That would remove the refund window entirely.

    Paying in parts: nobody pays for the group, and nobody has to find their own share at once. The payment link takes credit cards, card EMI and pay-later plans; any credit is between the payer and their lender, never Quorum.
